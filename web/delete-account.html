Delete your account and data

Privacy Policy · Terms of Service

In the app (immediate)

  1. Open BingeJam and go to Settings.
  2. Tap Delete account and confirm.
  3. If the app asks you to sign in again, do so and tap Delete account once more. Firebase requires a recent sign-in to delete an account.

Without the app

Email aritraditya.roy@gmail.com from the email address you signed in with, with the subject "Delete my BingeJam account" and your username. We delete it within 7 days and confirm by email.

What is deleted

  • your account, private account record (email, sign-in method) and your username, which becomes free for others;
  • your profile: display name, bio and profile picture;
  • your friends list (you're removed from your friends' lists too), friend requests, blocks, and join requests and invites you sent or received;
  • your direct-message chats, for both people in each chat;
  • your online status, activity and push tokens;
  • your membership of the room you're currently in, and its preview if you host it.

What may remain

  • Messages and reactions you posted in a watch-party or jam room chat that other people are still in. They stay visible to that room's members with the display name you had at the time, and are deleted with the room when everyone has left.
  • Reports you sent about other people or groups, until we've reviewed them.
  • Other people's block lists may still contain your former account ID, which no longer links to anything.
  • Our providers (Google Firebase, Cloudflare) may keep data in their own short-term backups for a limited time.

Uninstalling the app removes everything stored on your phone but doesn't delete your account.

Vyro

Privacy Policy · Last updated 15 October 2026

Vyro lets friends watch Netflix, JioHotstar or Prime Video together, each on their own Android phone, by keeping play, pause and position in sync, and lets friends see what each other is watching or listening to when they choose to share it. This policy explains what the app reads, what it stores and why. Vyro is not affiliated with Netflix, JioHotstar, Amazon, Spotify or YouTube.

Your account

  • You sign in with Google or with a one-time sign-in link sent to your email. We receive your email address, and from Google also your name and profile photo address.
  • Your email address is private: it's stored only for your own account and is never shown to other users.
  • You choose a unique username, a display name and optionally a short bio and a profile picture (a photo or GIF you pick). These form your public profile, which other signed-in Vyro users can see and find by username. If you sign in with Google, your Google profile photo may be used until you change or remove it.

What Vyro reads on your phone

  • Media sessions of supported apps (Netflix, JioHotstar, Prime Video, Spotify, YouTube Music, Amazon Music, YouTube): whether something is playing or paused, the position, which controls the app supports, and the title, subtitle, length and artwork the app publishes for Android's media controls. In a watch party, Vyro sends the video app play, pause and seek commands. Android only allows this for apps with Notification Access, which is why Vyro asks for it.
  • Vyro never reads your notifications, messages or their content, and never records or transmits your screen, video or audio.
  • Clipboard, once: on its very first launch the app checks the clipboard one time for a Vyro invite link (the invite page puts it there so a fresh install can open your friend's room). Anything else is ignored; nothing from it is stored or sent. Android shows its own notice when this happens.

Activity sharing (you choose)

  • Sharing is off for every app until you turn it on, app by app (for example Netflix on, Spotify off). You can change this or go invisible at any time in Settings.
  • For the apps you share, your friends only see what you're playing: the app, title, subtitle, a small artwork thumbnail, whether it's playing, and whether you're hosting a watch party. They also see whether you're online and when you were last seen. It's cleared when you stop playing, go invisible or go offline.

What is stored, and where

Profile pictures you upload are stored on Cloudflare (Workers KV) and served from a public link that only appears on your profile; removing your picture deletes it. Everything else is stored in Google Firebase (Realtime Database, Singapore region):

  • your profile (username, display name, photo address, bio) and private account record (email, sign-in method);
  • your friends, friend requests you send or receive, and people you've blocked;
  • messages you send to friends, readable only by you and that friend, and when you last read each chat;
  • your online status and, if you share it, your current activity (above);
  • "ask to join" requests and room invites between friends;
  • a push token for each phone you're signed in on, so we can notify you about new messages and friend requests (removed when you sign out);
  • for watch parties: room membership, the room's play/pause state and position, your periodically reported position (used for syncing), the host's now-playing title, length and artwork thumbnail, room chat and reactions. A room's contents are readable only by its members; a room's preview can be read by any signed-in user who has the room code, so invitees can see what's playing before joining.

Other network requests

  • Update check: once per launch the app asks GitHub's public API whether a newer version exists. No personal data is sent; GitHub sees the request (IP address) as with any web request.
  • Notifications: when you message a friend or send a friend request, the app asks our notification relay (hosted on Cloudflare) to notify them. The relay checks it's really you, reads the message or request from our database and hands it to Google's Firebase Cloud Messaging for delivery. It stores nothing.
  • The invite web page uses no trackers, ads, analytics or third-party scripts.

Vyro contains no ads and no analytics SDKs, and never sells or shares your data.

Optional permissions

  • Notifications: new messages and friend requests, and one quiet notification while you're in a watch party so Android keeps syncing running. Turn either kind off in Android's notification settings.
  • Display over other apps: only if you turn on the floating chat bubble.
  • Battery optimisation exemption: only if you allow it, so syncing isn't stopped mid-movie.

Your choices and deletion

  • Turn activity sharing off per app, go invisible, unfriend or block anyone at any time.
  • Signing out stops sharing and marks you offline. Uninstalling removes everything stored on your phone.
  • To delete your account and its data (profile, username, friends, messages, activity), contact us at the address below from the email you signed in with.

Children

Vyro is intended for users aged 18 and over and is not directed at children.

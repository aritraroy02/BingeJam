Sync it

Privacy Policy · Last updated 8 October 2026

Sync it lets friends watch Netflix or JioHotstar together, each on their own Android phone, by keeping play, pause and position in sync. This policy explains what the app reads, what it stores and why. Sync it is not affiliated with Netflix or JioHotstar.

What Sync it reads on your phone

  • Media session of the streaming app you choose (Netflix or JioHotstar): whether it's playing or paused, the playback position and speed, which controls it supports, and the title, subtitle, length and artwork it publishes for Android's media controls. Sync it sends that app play, pause and seek commands. Android only allows this for apps with Notification Access, which is why Sync it asks for it.
  • Sync it never reads your notifications, messages or their content, and never records or transmits your screen, video or audio.
  • Clipboard, once: on its very first launch the app checks the clipboard one time for a Sync it invite link (the invite web page puts it there so a fresh install can open your friend's room). Anything else on the clipboard is ignored; nothing from it is stored or sent. Android shows its own "pasted from clipboard" notice when this happens.

What is stored, and where

Rooms are stored in Google Firebase (Realtime Database, Singapore region) using an anonymous sign-in. No account, email or phone number is needed. For each room we store:

  • an anonymous user ID and the display name you enter;
  • room membership, online status and when you were last seen;
  • the room's play/pause state and position, and your periodically reported position (used for syncing);
  • if you host: the title, subtitle, length and a small artwork thumbnail of what you're playing, and any share link you choose to add;
  • chat messages and reactions you send in the room.

A room's contents are readable only by its members. A room's preview (host name, platform, title, length and artwork thumbnail) can be read by any Sync it user who has the room code, so invitees can see what's playing before joining; previews can't be listed or searched.

Other network requests

  • Update check: once per launch the app asks GitHub's public API whether a newer version exists. No personal data is sent; GitHub sees the request (IP address) as with any web request.
  • The invite web page (syncit-watchparty.web.app) uses no trackers, ads, analytics or third-party scripts.

Sync it contains no ads and no analytics SDKs, and never sells or shares your data.

Optional permissions

  • Notifications: one quiet notification while you're in a room, so Android keeps syncing running.
  • Display over other apps: only if you turn on the floating chat bubble.
  • Battery optimisation exemption: only if you allow it, so syncing isn't stopped mid-movie.

Your choices and deletion

  • Leaving a room removes your membership from it. You can stop syncing at any time from the app or its notification.
  • Uninstalling the app removes everything stored on your phone, including your anonymous ID.
  • To have room data (chat, name, positions) deleted from our database, contact us at the address below with the room code.

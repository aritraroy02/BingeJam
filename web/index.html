Sync it

Watch Netflix or JioHotstar together, each on your own phone. When anyone plays, pauses or skips, everyone's movie follows.

Download for Android
  1. Open the downloaded file to install it. If Android asks, allow your browser to install apps.
  2. Create a room and share the invite link with friends.

Sync it shares play, pause and position with your room, never your video, audio or notifications.

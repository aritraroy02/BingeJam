BingeJam

Same movie, same second, different homes

Watch Netflix or JioHotstar together, each on your own phone. When anyone plays, pauses or skips, everyone's movie follows.

This in-app browser can't install apps. Open this page in Chrome to get BingeJam. Open in Chrome

BingeJam is for Android phones. Scan this with your phone's camera to get it from the PhonePe Appstore.

Installing BingeJam

  1. BingeJam opens on the PhonePe Appstore. Tap Install.
  2. No PhonePe Appstore on your phone yet? The page shows how to get it, free, then come back to BingeJam.
  3. Open BingeJam and finish setup. Updates come through the PhonePe Appstore.
The store didn't open? Tap below to try again, or search BingeJam in the PhonePe Appstore. Open the PhonePe Appstore

Create a room in the app and share its invite link. BingeJam shares play, pause, position and what's playing with your room, never your video, audio or notifications.

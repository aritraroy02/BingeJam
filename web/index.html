Installing BingeJam

  1. BingeJam opens on the Indus Appstore. Tap Install.
  2. No Indus Appstore on your phone yet? The page shows how to get it, free, then come back to BingeJam.
  3. Open BingeJam and finish setup. Updates come through the Indus Appstore.

Installing on your PC

  1. Open BingeJam-Setup from your downloads. It installs in a few seconds, no admin rights needed.
  2. Windows may say Windows protected your PC because BingeJam isn't signed yet. Click More info, then Run anyway.
  3. Sign in with the same account as on your phone. Your friends, chats and groups are already there.
  4. Watching in a browser? Click Add to browser in the app so Netflix, Prime Video or JioHotstar stays in sync.

Installing BingeJam

  1. BingeJam opens on the PhonePe Appstore. Tap Install.
  2. No PhonePe Appstore on your phone yet? The page shows how to get it, free, then come back to BingeJam.
  3. Open BingeJam and finish setup. Updates come through the PhonePe Appstore.

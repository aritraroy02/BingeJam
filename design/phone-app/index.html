Suggestions

  1. Make the room one screen. Today the room splits Sync, People and Chat into tabs, so mid-movie you switch tabs to talk or check who's behind. Screen 5 puts the timeline, people and chat together; this is the biggest win.
  2. One "Watch together" sheet everywhere. Rooms start from a title, a friend, a chat or a group, each slightly differently. One sheet (pick the title if none, then who) would make every start feel the same.
  3. Ask for permissions when they're needed. Notification Access when you first start or join a room, the microphone when you first tap voice. Fewer people stop at a wall of permissions on day one.
  4. Design the end of a watch party. When a room ends, show "You watched Dune with Riya and Kabir · 2 h 46 min" with "Next episode together" for series. The watched-together card already exists; this makes it the last thing people see.
  5. Labels on the bottom tabs. Icon-only tabs make people guess (the TV icon could be Home or Watch). Short labels under the icons fix that.
  6. Title search on Home. Today Home only shows trending; people who know what they want to watch have no way in. The PC already has search.
  7. Accessibility pass while building: 48dp tap areas, TalkBack names on icon buttons and avatars, larger-text layouts (rows grow instead of clipping), reduced motion respected (the countdown fades instead of scaling).
  8. Keep the app ARM-only build in mind: WebRTC made the APK bigger; if size matters for Indus, a split per CPU (arm64, armeabi-v7a) halves the download.
